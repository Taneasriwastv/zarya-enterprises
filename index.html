FAQ

Common questions

Who have you supplied to before?

We have fulfilled 14 orders for State Bank of India (SBI) offices in New Delhi, covering festive gifting, keyboards and mice, earphones, biometric scanners, printers, furnishings and appliances.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

No fixed minimum. We've delivered single items like a printer or a refrigerator as well as bulk orders of 40+ gift sets. Bulk orders usually get better per-unit pricing.

Do you provide a GST invoice?

Yes. Every order comes with a proper GST tax invoice showing HSN codes and CGST/SGST or IGST as applicable. You get a proforma invoice before the order is confirmed.

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across Delhi NCR: Noida, Greater Noida, Delhi, Gurugram, Ghaziabad and Faridabad.

How long does delivery take?

Most in-stock items reach you within a few working days of approval. For large gifting orders or specific models we confirm the timeline in the quotation.

Can you source a specific brand or model?

Yes. Share the exact brand, model number or specification and we'll quote for that item, or suggest close alternatives if it's unavailable.

Are you registered with the government?

Zarya Enterprises is GST registered, Udyam registered as a micro enterprise (MSE) and a registered seller on the Government e-Marketplace (GeM).
